5. Let S(n) denote the statement “33 n  1  5n  2 is divisible by 22”.


                                                                     Since 34  53   44  22 ( 2), S(1) is true.
  Assume S(k) is true for some positive integer k, that is 33 k  1  5k  2  22a for some integer a.
  Consider S(k  1): 33 k  4  5k  3  27 33 k  1  5 5k  2  27(22a  5k  2)  5 5k  2  22(27a  5k  2).
  In other words, S(k  1) is also true.
  By the Principle of Mathematical Induction, S(n) is true for all positive integers n.
